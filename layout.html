Technology · Santa Clara, California

HUANG IS BACK

Eleven days after resigning, Nvidia’s co-founder is chief executive again — voted back unanimously by the board, cheered by staff on his return, and rewarded by a leap of more than 30% in the share price.

THE COMEBACK THAT LIFTED THE MARKET Shares jumped on Monday — the stock’s third-best day on record — as AI shares led a broad rebound that lifted the whole market. President Trump said he “convinced” the board to restore Huang; the returning chief told staff: “Thank you for the second chance. I’m going to be a better CEO the second time around.”

Ukraine · War

Drones hit Russian warship off Libya; refinery ablaze

Kyiv says its drones struck a Russian warship at a naval base in Libya, and a refinery at Novoshakhtinsk is burning after another drone attack. Media reports, citing leaked government data, put Russia’s war death toll near 700,000.

United States · Immigration

ICE arrests at Seattle airport called ‘out of control’

Agents detained a father and daughter on their way to a family baptism, and a Princeton neuroscientist was held at Dulles after a conference. Lawmakers called the scenes appalling.

Iran · Diplomacy

Trump warns Iran of a ‘very traumatic’ outcome

The president said the result would be severe if nuclear talks fail. Washington sanctioned an Iranian underground banking network; Tehran warned against a US naval blockade.

Korea · Security

Jets scrambled as Russian planes near island

Several Russian military aircraft entered South Korea’s air defence zone near Ulleungdo, setting off an air raid alert that was lifted after five minutes. Seoul says its airspace was not violated.

Trade · Economy

Trump hails Japan deal as EU threatens $84bn tariffs

Washington announced trade pacts with Japan and Indonesia, while Brussels readied retaliation on $84 billion of US goods as negotiations stall.

Climate · COP31

Climate talks stall in Türkiye

Negotiators remain split over fossil fuels and finance for developing nations, days after the Antalya summit ended with no agreement at all.
